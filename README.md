## Project Overview

This project analyzes the lead-to-client conversion performance of Emerald Prime Properties across sales representatives, locations, and property categories. The dashboard provides management with visibility into lead generation, client acquisition, conversion efficiency, inspection activities, and product performance to support data-driven sales decisions.

---

# Business Problem

Emerald Prime Properties generates property leads through multiple sales representatives and locations. However, management lacks clear visibility into:

* Which sales representatives convert the highest number of leads into clients.
* The effectiveness of lead management and follow-up processes.
* Regional performance differences between Lagos and Rivers.
* Property categories generating the highest client conversions.
* The gap between generated leads and successfully converted clients.

Without this insight, sales resources may not be allocated efficiently, leading to missed revenue opportunities and lower conversion rates.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this analysis were to:

1. Measure overall sales conversion performance.
2. Track lead generation and client acquisition metrics.
3. Identify high-performing and low-performing sales representatives.
4. Analyze conversion performance by location.
5. Evaluate the impact of property inspections on client conversion.
6. Determine which property categories drive the most client acquisitions.
7. Provide actionable recommendations to improve sales efficiency and revenue growth.

# Analysis Process

## 1. Data Collection

* Gathered sales lead and client data from Emerald Prime Properties.
* Included sales representative performance records.
* Included regional and property category information.

## 2. Data Cleaning

* Removed inconsistencies and duplicates.
* Standardized salesperson and location information.
* Validated lead and client counts.

## 3. Data Transformation

* Created calculated fields for:

  * Conversion Rate
  * Pending Clients
  * Inspection Percentage
  * Regional Performance Metrics

## 4. Dashboard Development

* Designed KPI cards for quick performance tracking.
* Built interactive filters for:

  * Sales Representatives
  * Locations
  * Property Categories
* Created charts to visualize sales funnel performance.

## 5. Performance Analysis

* Compared lead generation versus client acquisition.
* Evaluated regional performance.
* Assessed salesperson effectiveness.
* Measured inspection contribution to conversions.

# Key Insights

## Overall Sales Performance

| KPI             | Value |
| --------------- | ----- |
| Total Leads     | 22    |
| Total Clients   | 5     |
| Pending Clients | 17    |
| Conversion Rate | 22.7% |

### Insight

Only 22.7% of generated leads were converted into paying clients, while 77.3% remain unconverted, indicating a significant opportunity to improve lead nurturing and follow-up processes.

---

## Sales Representative Performance

### Lead Generation Leaders

* Funmi generated approximately 49 leads, the highest among all sales representatives.
* Chioma followed closely with approximately 48 leads.
* Chukwudi generated approximately 43 leads.

### Client Conversion Leaders

* Funmi converted approximately 28 clients, the highest conversion volume.
* Chioma converted approximately 20 clients.
* Chukwudi converted approximately 18 clients.

### Insight

Funmi demonstrates the strongest overall sales performance by leading both lead generation and client acquisition, making her sales approach a potential benchmark for the team.

---

## Regional Performance

| Location | Share |
| -------- | ----- |
| Lagos    | 50.8% |
| Rivers   | 35.5% |

### Insight

Lagos contributes the largest proportion of business activity and represents the strongest market opportunity for client acquisition and revenue growth.

---

## Inspection Analysis

| Metric                     | Value |
| -------------------------- | ----- |
| Inspection Completion Rate | 38.5% |

### Insight

Less than half of generated leads progressed to property inspections. Since inspections are a critical stage in the property buying journey, increasing inspection rates could significantly improve conversions.

---

## Gender Analysis

| Metric         | Value |
| -------------- | ----- |
| Female Clients | 22.7% |

### Insight

Female clients represent a relatively small proportion of the customer base, suggesting potential opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns.

---

## Product Performance

| Property Type | Client Share |
| ------------- | ------------ |
| Residential   | 64.6%        |
| Commercial    | 34.6%        |
| Industrial    | 0.0%         |

### Insights

* Residential properties dominate client interest and account for nearly two-thirds of successful conversions.
* Commercial properties contribute over one-third of client acquisitions.
* Industrial properties generated no measurable client conversions.

# Recommendations

## 1. Improve Lead Follow-Up Strategy

* Implement structured follow-up schedules.
* Introduce CRM reminders and lead tracking.
* Prioritize high-intent leads.

### Expected Outcome

Increase conversion rate beyond the current 22.7%.

---

## 2. Increase Property Inspection Bookings

* Encourage sales representatives to move leads quickly into inspection stages.
* Automate inspection scheduling where possible.

### Expected Outcome

Higher inspection-to-client conversion rates.

---

## 3. Replicate High-Performer Strategies

* Analyze Funmi's sales process and client engagement techniques.
* Train other representatives using proven best practices.

### Expected Outcome

Improved team-wide conversion performance.

---

## 4. Focus Marketing Efforts on Lagos

* Allocate more advertising and sales resources to Lagos.
* Expand lead generation campaigns in high-performing regions.

### Expected Outcome

Higher lead volume and increased revenue generation.

---

## 5. Reassess Industrial Property Strategy

* Review pricing, marketing, and customer targeting for industrial properties.
* Determine whether demand exists within current target markets.

### Expected Outcome

Improved portfolio diversification and potential revenue growth.

---

## 6. Target Underrepresented Customer Segments

* Develop campaigns aimed at increasing female client engagement.
* Create tailored property offerings and marketing messages.

### Expected Outcome

Broader customer reach and improved market penetration.
